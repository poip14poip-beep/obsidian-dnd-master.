The Ekdrasil integration adapts the upstream tool-driven AI-DM architecture
(patterns for server-owned adjudication, dice handling, and separation between
mechanical outcomes and narration). It does not copy Lorekeeper's 2014/5e
rules dataset. Ekdrasil continues to use its separately maintained D&D 2024 /
SRD 5.2.1 source and Ekdrasil v2.4 canon.
